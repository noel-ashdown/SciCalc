SciCalc is a local add-in for Autodesk Fusion.
It does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data or usage information.
All data created by SciCalc (calculation history, variables, settings and bookmarks)
is stored exclusively in files on your local machine and is never transmitted to any external server or third party.
